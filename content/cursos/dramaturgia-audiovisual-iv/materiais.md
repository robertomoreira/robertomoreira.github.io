- CHION, Michel. *O roteiro de cinema*. São Paulo: Martins Fontes, 1989.

- MACKENDRICK, Alexander; CRONIN, Paul. *On Film-making: an introduction to the craft of the director*. London: Faber and Faber, 2004.
- MOLLICK, Ethan. *Co-Intelligence*. New York: Random House, 2024.
